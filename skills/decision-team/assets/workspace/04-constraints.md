# Constraints

Only things that genuinely cannot change within the decision window. A
preference recorded as a constraint removes an option from consideration without
anyone noticing it was removed.

## Budget
<Ceiling, and what happens if it is exceeded.>

## Time
<Deadline, and what is driving it. A date with no external driver is a
preference.>

## Team
<Who is available, and for what fraction of their time.>

## Legal / regulatory / contractual
<Obligations that bound the option space.>

## Technical
<Platform, integration, or architectural limits that are real rather than
inherited habit.>

## Explicitly out of scope
<Options ruled out, and by whom, and why. Worth recording: ruled-out options
have a way of being the right answer, and this makes the ruling visible enough
to challenge.>
