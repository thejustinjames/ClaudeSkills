# Hiring history

The Historian's slice. The funnel's own track record is evidence — fill this
from records, not memory, and cite items in 03-evidence.md.

## Comparable past hires
<For each hire at a similar level/shape: how they were found (channel), how
long to net-positive, outcome at 12–24 months, still here?>

## The regret pattern
<What the org regrets about past hires — and whether the signal that would
have caught it was available at decision time. Was it in the interviews, the
references, a trial nobody ran?>

## Non-standard hires
<What happened when the org hired someone who did not match the usual
profile — the real, revealed tolerance for difference, as opposed to the
stated one.>

## Rejected-pile audits
<Results of past audits (memo-format.md, standing card). If none has ever
run, say so — the false-negative rate is unmeasured, and the memo must not
imply the shortlist was exhaustive.>

## Time-to-productive base rate
<How long comparable hires actually took to carry their load — the number
the Cost & Capacity Analyst's ramp assumption gets checked against.>
