# Hard constraints and gates

Things that cannot change, plus the audited gates. Every role receives this
file — keep it to facts.

## Legitimate hard gates (references/gates-and-finding.md)
<Right to work, licence/clearance, language the work demands, genuine
location requirement. These — and only these — go at the top of any advert,
stated first.>

## Proxies demoted to soft signals
<Every years-of-experience, exact-title, credential, or keyword requirement
from the draft JD, each rewritten as the real thing it was standing for and
the evidence that would demonstrate it. A proxy used as a knockout after this
audit is a red-team finding.>

## Budget ceiling
<The number above which the answer is no, regardless of candidate.>

## Legal and policy constraints
<Jurisdiction-specific employment rules in play, internal comp policy,
approval chains. Anything with legal exposure goes to counsel — noted here,
not resolved here.>

## Timing constraints
<Freeze dates, notice periods, the point after which the hire misses its
purpose.>
